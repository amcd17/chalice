BASILICA · CONCEPT C

A church with a rotunda

The footprint is a church, not an oval. The oval walkway is the ring around the shrine at the crossing. Nave west, transept through the crossing, apse east, porch west, cloister south-west, tower north-west, chapel south, churchyard south-east. Spectator galleries sit above the aisles, blocked.

THE SHAPE

A long nave with two aisles, a transept through the crossing, a rounded apse, a west porch, a south-west cloister, a north-west tower, a south chapel and a south-east churchyard. No side mirrors another.

THE OVAL WALKWAY

The oval is the rotunda ring around the cup at the crossing. Six metres wide, ten columns, four openings onto the nave, the transept, the choir and the apse. A close-quarters loop at the objective, not the map boundary.

ROUTES

  • West porch into the nave.
  • Cloister door into the south aisle.
  • North and south transept doors.
  • Apse door behind the rotunda.
  • Four ways into the rotunda.

SEATING AND LEVELS

The spectator galleries are above the two aisles, tiered and blocked, facing the nave. There is no second playable floor. If one is wanted later, the choir loft over the porch is the natural place, not a ring.

PLAY

  • Seeker owns the nave and the transept arms.
  • Rascal uses the cloister, the chapel and the churchyard.
  • Crusader walls the rood screen and the rotunda openings.
  • Lampist holds the apse and the chapel.
  • Brigand rushes the transept and the porch.
