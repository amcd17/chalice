BASILICA · SECOND PASS

Two levels, closed centre

Ground floor gets a crossing room, screens, a colonnade and pew cover. The existing 12.5 m top tier becomes two three-bay galleries, joined by one broken bridge and one drop, so the upper floor is a loop rather than a ring.

The dais becomes a room. Two screens cut the long axis, the centre is no longer a clear run.

THE SECOND LEVEL

  • Two galleries on the existing 12.5 m top tier, three bays each, the middle bay set back 1.2 m with buttresses at the joints.
  • One bridge at the apse end at 12.5 m: 2.2 m wide, a 2.5 m broken span, a low parapet on one side and a choir box at the near end.
  • One drop at the choir end to the tier at 5 m. With the chapel stairs, that makes a loop, not a ring.
  • Banners between the bays cut the cross-arena line. The screens rise to 13 m with a rood loft, so the galleries cannot see the dais.

WHAT STAYS

  • The elliptical shell, the dome, the rose window and the chandelier line.
  • The four chapels, the raised floors and ramps. Each keeps its tier door and gains a stair to the gallery.
  • The tier at 5 m as the first ring, with a taller parapet and buttress bays.
