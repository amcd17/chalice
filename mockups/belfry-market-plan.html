Belfry Market

A village square with a fallen bell tower, a night market, and a catacomb under both · about 96 × 68 m

The idea

A village square where the bell tower has fallen across into the market. The chalice sits on a dais in the square, right under the bell hanging from the fallen tip.

Thistle · the churchyard

  • Spawns inside the church, west: about 20 × 40 m, a nave with two aisles of pillars, so the spawn itself has cover. Big doors open east toward the lychgate. The south wall has collapsed into a rubble slope that runs down about 2 m and out to tanners' lane, the back way to the market.
  • Owns the height: the fallen tower is a ramp from 8 m down to 4 m, looking straight down onto the dais.
  • Weak at close range: the churchyard has some cover (yew, tall tombs, lychgate), but nothing like the market's alleys.

Marigold · the market

  • Spawns in the covered market hall, east.
  • Owns the cover: stall rows, lantern alleys and tanners' lane, all short sightlines.
  • Owns its own height: an upper gallery inside the market hall, with a balcony about 4 m up that looks over the square and the top of tanners' lane. Lower than the tower, so Thistle still has the best view.
  • Shares the catacomb: the ossuary stair leads down, and a ladder climbs to a cellar hatch on the market side of the square.

The counters

  • Marigold can climb a scaffold onto the tower tip and fight for the high ground.
  • The catacomb is evenly contested. Thistle's crypt reaches the well, and Marigold's ossuary reaches the hatch, each about 16 m. Both teams meet in the cult chamber under the dais: a big candle-lit room with a ritual circle and four pillars for cover.
  • An open grate in tanners' lane drops straight down into the chamber's south passage. It's one way, a fast route in from the market side but no way back up.
  • Thistle's tower and Marigold's gallery can see each other across the square, so each team's height can be challenged.
  • The south slope works both ways: Thistle's flank out to tanners' lane and the market, and Marigold's way into the church from the south.

Cover (v2)

  • Something to duck behind every 8 to 10 m on every route. The only truly open spot is the ring around the dais, so grabbing the chalice stays a risk.
  • Tower lane: rubble from the fall and a broken belfry section you can walk through.
  • Square: market cross, bell frame, toppled stall, carts and crates.
  • The ramp has a gap to jump and a low wall, so no single spot on the tower sees the whole square.

Lanes

  • North: the fallen tower (high) and the open lane beneath it.
  • Middle: the square, carts and crates for cover.
  • South: tanners' lane, covered stalls.
  • Under: the catacomb, crypt to ossuary. Two ways up: the well (Thistle's side) and the hatch (Marigold's side).

Fair start

Both spawns are about 30 m from the dais. Thistle's quickest path is the gate, Marigold's is the hall door.

Optional

Hit the bell and it tolls across the map, which gives the shooter's position away. A Bellringer could use it on purpose.
