BELLRINGER HEADWEAR

Nine ways to cover his face. Same body, Thistle colours.

1 · BRONZE HELM

A bronze helmet with a rounded skull, a low ridge and a short neck flare. Open at the front in the T of an ancient Corinthian helm, a dark face inside. A bell-rope loop on the crown is the only hint of the bell.

2 · BELFRY LOUVRES

A hood with a mask of wooden slats, like a bell tower's sound shutters. Clever, and reads as "bell tower" to anyone who has seen one.

3 · MUFFLED

The face fully wrapped in stitched cloth under his cap: the ear binding taken all the way. Creepy, and echoes the Lampist's bandages.

4 · LIRIPIPE AND MASK

A minstrel's hood with a long trailing tail, and a pale mask with closed painted eyes and a round singing mouth. The most "musician".

5 · VEILED

A broad hat with a dark mourning veil over the face. A funeral bellringer: tolling for the dead.

6 · TRICKSTER'S COWL

A jester's two-point cowl with a little bronze bell on each tip, and a small bronze half-mask over the eyes. The mouth shows: a smirk. Light, fast, nothing a soldier would wear.

7 · BORROWED POT

A dented kettle helm two sizes too big, tipped over one eye on a frayed strap, with a cloth rag knotted under it. Reads as scavenged, not drilled.

8 · SCARF AND BELL CAP

A floppy cap with one long tail and a bell on the end, and a cream scarf wound over the nose and mouth. Eyes show, narrow and dark.

9 · COWL AND T-MASK

The jester cowl and bells, with the helm's bronze T-front worn as a mask. Dark face behind the T, bell-rope loop dropped. The soldier's face with a trickster's hat.
