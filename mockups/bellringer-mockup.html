THE BELLRINGER

Mockup, 23 Nov. Trapper. 125 HP. Bell-bow, tuning forks, Alarm Bell, Bell Rope.

THE MINSTREL

Two-colour tunic split down the middle, hose swapped side to side, long pointed shoes, a round cap. Ears bound with cloth: bellringers went deaf. Three tuning forks in a sheath at the hip, a hand bell on the belt, the bell-bow across his back.

THE BELL-BOW

The bell sits in the oval cradle at the string's centre. Hold to draw (1 s for a full note): the striker comes back toward you. Release: it snaps into the bell and a ring of sound leaves it. Quick strike, about 35 up close; full note, about 75, travelling at a visible speed.

HIS KIT IN THE WORLD

Bell Rope: yoke bells at both ends, the rope between their tops; the far end can be higher, lower or level. The spike of light is the aim preview. Tuning fork (one of three) humming in the floor. Alarm Bell: the Marking Lantern's throw and stats, as a small tinkling bell.
