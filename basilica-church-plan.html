BASILICA · CONCEPT B

A church, not a bowl

The oval walkway stays as the outer loop. The church is offset north-west, the cloister sits south-west, the transept runs south, the apse east, and the churchyard fills the south-east. The seating outside the walkway is spectator space, blocked.

THE SHAPE

The oval walkway is the outer rotation route, unchanged. The church inside it is deliberately off-centre: nave and north aisle to the north-west, cloister to the south-west, transept to the south, apse to the east. No side mirrors another, so the map cannot be read as a bowl.

THE ROUTES

  • Walkway: the full oval loop, with four doors to the churchyard and the church.
  • Churchyard: open ground south-east with graves and trees, a flank between the walkway and the transept.
  • Porch and north aisle: two ways into the nave, so the west end is not a single choke.
  • Transept south door: the short way to the crossing from the churchyard.
  • Apse door: the east way in, behind the cup.

THE UPPER LEVEL

One choir loft over the west nave at 8 m, reached by a stair from the porch. It looks down the nave and across to the crossing, but it is a platform, not a ring. Spectator galleries over the aisles stay blocked. The loft is the only new upper floor, and it cannot circle the map.

PLAY

  • Seeker holds the nave and the walkway sightlines.
  • Rascal uses the cloister, churchyard and walkway.
  • Crusader walls the porch, the rood screen and the transept door.
  • Lampist holds the apse and the chapel.
  • Brigand rushes the transept and the churchyard.

BUDGETS

  • Nave line 40 m, broken by the rood screen.
  • Walkway loop continuous, 8 m wide.
  • Every church door within 12 m of cover.
  • Cup at the crossing, three ways in.
